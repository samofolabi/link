Why Aaru

The same pattern, three times

Something complex becomes something small enough to build on. I have done this twice already. Here is what the third time would look like.

Janerate and Sama are finished work. Aaru's row is dashed on purpose, it is a proposal, not a claim.

The problem, as I read it

Aaru's real problem is not the simulation itself, it is the distance between what research ships and what a customer can act on. That is the exact shape of problem I keep choosing.

Janerate

I turned a complex set of payment and registration operations into a small number of capability scoped commands anyone can call safely, without needing to understand the financial logic underneath.

Sama

I turned whether to trust an AI agent's own judgment into a single bounded threshold check, so nothing downstream has to reason about the model underneath it.

Aaru

This role is asking for that same skill applied to something harder, taking a genuinely difficult research capability and making it small and safe enough that a customer facing workflow can depend on it every time. I want to build that translation layer, and I want to build it somewhere the stakes are real enough that getting the abstraction wrong actually matters.
